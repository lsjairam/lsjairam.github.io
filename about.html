About · Engineering practice

Keeping the work understandable.

I work between site conditions and project records, helping teams see what changed, what matters, and what needs to happen next.

01 / Approach

How I work

Site to office

Clear information is a construction tool.

My day-to-day work covers RFIs, submittals, drawing revisions, supplier schedules, delivery records, billings, and technical coordination. These can look like administrative tasks, but together they determine whether a team can act confidently and whether a project can explain its own decisions later.

I prefer systems that are simple enough to use under real project pressure, structured enough to remain traceable, and flexible enough to fit how a team actually works.

01 · Trace

Connect each issue to its source, responsible party, evidence, and resolution.

02 · Coordinate

Turn scattered information into specific next actions for the people involved.

03 · Verify

Close the loop with records that show what was inspected and what changed.

02 / Background

Built from the fundamentals

Engineer, coordinator, systems builder.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering at Bulacan State University in 2024 and became a registered civil engineer in 2025. I now support two active building projects in Bulacan as a Document Controller Engineer at Total Safe Development Construction, Inc.

My earlier work with the DPWH Bulacan 2nd District Engineering Office grounded me in site investigations, surveying, AutoCAD land plotting, bidding documents, and the discipline of maintaining dependable construction records.
